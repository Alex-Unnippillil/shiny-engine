    WHY LOOKLOCK?

    Give the scene a new look.
    Not the scoreboard.

    A creator can keep the original HUD, caption, or slide label while reviewing a neural treatment of the surrounding scene. Compare before publishing, and save a reproducible recipe for that exact pair.

    BRING YOUR OWN OUTPUT

    A companion to your renderer.

    Import a processed still from a tool you are authorized to use. LookLock composes and reviews it locally; it does not invoke that tool or authenticate its model. Built-in OpenDLSS-NR inference remains gated in Neural Lab.
